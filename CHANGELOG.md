# Changelog

All notable changes to StickMan Gen. Versions are tagged on GitHub (`v1.0`, `v1.1`, …).

## v1.1 (2026-09-29)

- Renamed from Stickman Duel to **StickMan Gen**. Added a footer with the version, "Made by Edmond Yang" and a GitHub link.
- **Simple move list:** "Your fight" shows moves as numbered cards in play order, replacing the time-scaled timeline. Tap a card to jump to it, and drag or use Earlier/Later to reorder.
- **Copy as text:** copies the fight as a time-coded video prompt for AI video tools such as Seedance. Fighters are called Fighter One, Two and so on, and fights over 15 s are split into clips.
- **Random** makes armed fighters favour their weapon moves and finish with a weapon KO.
- Live at https://stickmangen.vercel.app. Added the README, the MIT license and the repo files.

## v1.0

- First release as **Stickman Duel**: 40 chainable moves, up to 4 fighters, sword and axe, synthesized sound, and MP4/WebM export.
